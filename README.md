## Meet the crew

The Kitchen Mafia is a crew of chefs and hosts based in Johannesburg, cooking since 2022. We started with a simple rule: if it leaves our kitchen, we'd put our name on it.

Today that means private dinners, corporate functions, celebrations and everything in between. You get restaurant-level plating, honest portions and a team that shows up ready.

- Made fresh to order - no reheated shortcuts
- Plated with care - from sushi platters to lamb racks
- Reliable on the day - we handle the details so you don't have to
